Official Rules & Guidelines

  • Women (3-6 players)
  • Each game consists of 10 frames.
  • Players must observe all safety rules of the bowling center.
  • Players must handle bowling balls carefully.
  • Players should avoid stepping onto the approach area when another player is preparing to bowl.
  • The player with the highest total score shall be declared the winner.
  • Players must wear proper sports attire.
