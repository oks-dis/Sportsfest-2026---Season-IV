Volleyball Competition Mechanics

Official Rules & Guidelines
  • Each team shall have a minimum of 12 players and a maximum of 15 players.
  • Each team must have both male and female players.
  • Each team shall have six (6) players on the court.
  • Each team shall designate a Team Captain.
  • The volleyball competition shall be played using a Best-of-5 sets format.
  • The first team to win three (3) sets wins the match.
  • A team must win a set by at least two (2) points.
  • The 5th set, when necessary, shall be played up to 15 points, with a minimum two-point advantage.
  • Players must wear appropriate sports attire and volleyball shoes.
